# Ludo King Lite

A browser-based Ludo game built with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Play locally with friends on one screen or add simple bot opponents. Includes lightweight sound effects and a kid-friendly layout.

## Features
- Up to 4 players with any mix of humans and bots (auto-fills to at least two seats).
- Dice rolling, captures, extra turn on six, and home stretch to the center.
- Click tokens to move them after rolling; bots move automatically.
- Roll button locks until you move, preventing accidental or repeated rolls.
- Sound toggle and quick rules on the page.

## Getting started
1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Choose the number of humans and robots, then press **New Game**.
3. Press **Roll 🎲** to start the turn. If you roll a 6 you can leave the yard and roll again.

No build tools are required—everything runs directly in the browser.
